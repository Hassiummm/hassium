<section class="block" id="introduction">
    <h2>Introduction</h2>
    <p>This page is the homepage of an informal reading group organized by me and Derek. The reading group will run from December 21, 2026 to January 17, 2027.
    <br>
    The idea of noncommutative geometry was established by Isarel Gelfand. In 1940s, he proved a correspondence, which was later known as Gelfand duality, between compact Hausdorff spaces and commutative unital $C^\ast$-algebras. Gelfand duality suggests that noncommutative algebra can be studied by the algebra of functions on a noncommutative space. The theory was formalized by Alain Connes during 1970s and 1980s. Connes extended the classical geometric concepts to noncommutative algebras.
    <br>
    The goal of this reading group is to study the duality between geometry and algebra from an **algebraic** point of view. In particular, we will study how spaces, maps, and geometric structures can be described through algebras of functions, ideals, derivations, and modules, and how these descriptions motivate their noncommutative counterparts.
    </p>
