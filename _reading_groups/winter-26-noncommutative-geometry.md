<section class="block" id="introduction">
<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p>This is the homepage of an informal reading group organized by Derek and me. The reading group will run from December 21, 2026, to January 17, 2027.</p>

<p>One of the mathematical foundations of noncommutative geometry was established by Israel Gelfand in the 1940s. He established a correspondence, now known as Gelfand duality, between compact Hausdorff spaces and commutative unital $C^\ast$-algebras. This correspondence suggests that a noncommutative algebra can be regarded as the algebra of functions on a hypothetical noncommutative space. The theory was further developed during the 1970s and 1980s, particularly through the work of Alain Connes, who extended classical geometric concepts to noncommutative algebras.</p>

<p>The goal of this reading group is to study the duality between geometry and algebra from an <strong>algebraic</strong> perspective. In particular, we will explore how spaces, maps, and geometric structures can be described through algebras of functions, ideals, derivations, and modules, and how these descriptions motivate their noncommutative counterparts.</p>
</section>
